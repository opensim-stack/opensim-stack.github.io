1 Open Ports

By default the stack exposes the following ports on your host. Forward them through your firewall/router if you want others to connect.

PortProtocolServicePurpose
9000TCP & UDPOpenSimulatorRegion simulator HTTP and UDP traffic.
Default passwords are intentionally lax for local use. Change all credentials to strong passwords and/or bearer tokens where required. Note, the stack automatically uses strong passwords for the bots and opencode instances.

2 Join the Hypergrid

OpenSimulator supports the Hypergrid, a protocol that lets avatars teleport between independent grids as if they were one big metaverse. Once your grid is reachable on a public hostname, other users can visit by teleporting to a hypergrid address like:

<OPENSIM_HOSTNAME>:9000:Region Name

To travel from your grid to another, open the world map in your viewer and enter the destination hypergrid address.
